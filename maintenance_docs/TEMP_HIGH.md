
# SOP-TEMP-001: High Equipment Temperature

Alarm Code: TEMP_HIGH
Equipment Type: Manufacturing Process Equipment
Severity: HIGH
Revision: 1.0
Document Status: SIMULATED / TRAINING ONLY

## Description
The equipment temperature has exceeded its configured
operating threshold. This condition may affect equipment
performance, process stability, and product quality.

## Possible Causes
- Insufficient cooling water flow
- Cooling fan malfunction
- Temperature sensor failure
- Excessive equipment load
- Cooling system obstruction

## Investigation Procedure
1. Review the temperature trend before the alarm.
2. Compare readings from available temperature sensors.
3. Check cooling water flow and pressure indicators.
4. Review cooling fan status and related alarm history.
5. Check whether equipment utilization increased
   before the temperature alarm.
6. Escalate abnormal readings to maintenance engineering.

## Recommended Data Analysis
- Compare temperature readings before and after downtime.
- Identify repeated TEMP_HIGH alarms on the same equipment.
- Analyze correlations between equipment load and temperature.
- Review historical downtime associated with temperature alarms.

## Escalation Criteria
- Temperature remains outside approved operating limits.
- Multiple temperature sensors report inconsistent readings.
- Repeated TEMP_HIGH alarms occur after recovery.
- Equipment protection systems activate.

## Production Lot Handling
Affected production lots must remain on HOLD until reviewed
by authorized production and quality personnel.

## Safety
Follow approved site safety and equipment isolation procedures.
Do not bypass equipment interlocks or protective systems.
